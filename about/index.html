About

The sketch is the promise. The piece keeps it.

Haldane is one bench in one room. Rings, bespoke watches and leather straps are drawn, made and finished here by the same hands, in the order the commissions arrive.

Every piece starts on paper. A ring is drawn at its true size, from the top and from the side, before any metal is cut; a watch dial is drawn at ten times its size so the hands can be judged; a strap is drawn around the lugs of the watch it will carry. The drawing stays with the piece and is delivered with it.

We work in 18 kt gold, platinum and vegetable-tanned leather, and we fit Swiss hand-wound movements into cases made to the wrist that will wear them. Nothing is kept in stock and nothing is rushed: a ring takes six to eight weeks, a watch ten to twelve, a strap two to three.
